# Bill C-283 — Somali Heritage Month Act

## Summary

This enactment designates the month of July as “Somali Heritage Month”.

## Short Title

### Section 1
*Short title*

This Act may be cited as the Somali Heritage Month Act .

## Somali Heritage Month

### Section 2
*Somali Heritage Month*

Throughout Canada, in each year, the month of July is to be known as “Somali Heritage Month”.
