# Bill C-287 — An Act to amend the National Research Council Act

## Summary

This enactment amends theto provide that, in its role supporting the national model code development system, the National Research Council must prepare, maintain and publish the following: National Research Council Act
(a) standardized housing cost and impact summaries for material housing-related model code proposals to be used in the public review of those proposals;
(b) governance transparency information respecting such proposals, including relevant committee memberships, roles and institutional or organizational affiliations; and
(c) an accessible public registry of such proposals and associated materials that includes edition-level and annual summaries of code development activities and impacts.
