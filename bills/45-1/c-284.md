# Bill C-284 — An Act to amend the Canada Labour Code (replacement workers)

## Summary

This enactment amends theto provide that, during a strike or lockout, an employer cannot use the services of any person who performs management functions or who is employed in a confidential capacity in matters related to industrial relations in another workplace to perform the duties of an employee who is in the bargaining unit on strike or locked out.
