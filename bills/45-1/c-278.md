# Bill C-278 — An Act to amend the Parliament of Canada Act

## Summary

This enactment amends theto provide that a member’s seat in the House of Commons is vacated and a by-election called for that seat if the member, having been elected to the House as a member of a registered party or as an independent, changes parties or becomes a member of a party, as the case may be.
